About

This is a story about Lina a girl fighting a battle with cancer and Dave a guy who lives life as a care free risk taker. Lina, who's life has been centered around care and quiteness meets Dave who's been living in total carelesness and noise. The two end up catching feelings for one another and learn the better side of their oposite lifes.

Lina

Reserved, thoughtful, battling cancer. Finds peace in books and quiet moments by the lake.

Dave

Carefree, energetic risk-taker. Lives for adrenaline, skateboarding, and making a splash.

Fredom's Last Breath

The beeping of machines echoed softly in the hospital room as Dave sat beside Lina’s bed, flipping through the pages of a worn novel. Lina, pale and fragile, leaned back against the pillows, her gaze fixed on the words as Dave read aloud. It had been three days since she was admitted for intense pain and monitoring, and during that time, Dave had stepped into her world, a world of quiet moments and measured care. To his surprise, he didn’t hate it. The stillness offered a kind of peace he hadn’t realized he needed. Sharing Lina’s love for books, laughing at the stories she adored, and simply being present with her brought a warmth he hadn’t felt in years.

On the fourth morning, Lina’s strength began to return. They sat together, the sunlight streaming through the window, painting golden streaks on the walls. Lina turned to Dave, her lips curving into a weak but determined smile. “I want to try it,” she said suddenly. Dave looked up from the book, confused. “Try what?” “Skating,” she replied. “I want to feel the freedom you promised me.” His jaw dropped, and then a wide grin spread across his face. “Are you serious?” When she nodded, he sprang to his feet, hastily packing her books. “Alright, let’s go. You’re about to have your mind blown.”



As they prepared to leave, Lina’s mother stepped into the room, her expression one of concern and fear. “Lina, you can’t leave the ward. The doctors” “Please, Mom,” Lina interrupted, her voice pleading. “I just have one thing to do before everything turns dark.” Tears welled up in her mother’s eyes as she looked at her daughter, then at Dave, who stood quietly by the door. Finally, with a sob, she stepped aside, watching them disappear down the corridor.

The skate park beside the lake was quiet, the golden hour casting long shadows over the ramps and rails. Dave stood still for a moment, memories flooding his mind. Though it had only been nine weeks since they’d met, it felt like years. “Dave!” Lina’s voice broke through his thoughts. She stood a few steps away, arms crossed and an amused smirk on her face. “Are we doing this or what?”. He laughed, running to her. “Alright, let’s do it.” Helping her onto the skateboard, he gently guided her feet. “Okay, just keep your balance. Bend your knees a little. You’ve got this.” As he prepared to give her a light push, she turned her head to him, her eyes sparkling.Hope You'll Catch Me, Dave.” He placed a reassuring hand on her shoulder. “Always.” With that, he pushed her gently. Lina wobbled at first, but as she felt the wind rush past her face, a smile spread across her lips. Tears streamed down her cheeks, blending with the wind. Dave ran alongside her, cheering loudly. “You’re doing it, Lina! You’re amazing!”

The moment felt perfect, almost otherworldly, until Lina’s movements became unsteady. Her vision blurred, and her knees buckled. Dave noticed the shift instantly and sprinted to her, catching her just as she collapsed. “I got you,” he whispered, cradling her in his arms. His voice cracked as he repeated, “I told you I’d catch you.” Lina’s breathing was shallow, her eyes fluttering open briefly. “Thank you,” she whispered, her voice faint but full of gratitude. Then, with one final exhale, she slipped away.

The hospital room was somber as the doctors informed Lina’s parents of her passing. Dave stood silently in the hallway, holding her mother tightly as she wept. His own tears fell freely, a storm of grief and love overwhelming him. Moments he hoped would never come.

The with the light of the sun entering the funeral home, but black remained it’s dominant shade in the home. At the funeral, Dave stood beside Lina’s casket, staring at her peaceful face. Memories of her laugh, her strength, and the way she lit up even the darkest days played in his mind like a reel of bittersweet moments. Tears ran down his cheeks as Lina’s mother approached him. “Dave,” she said softly, her voice trembling. “You were the best thing to happen to her. Thank you for making her happy.” Unable to speak, Dave nodded, pulling her into a gentle embrace.

Six months later, Dave stood by the lake, staring out over the water. The bench where Lina used to sit was empty, but her presence lingered in every ripple of the lake and every rustle of the trees. In his hand was a skateboard, her name painted across it in bold, bright letters. He closed his eyes, letting the memories wash over him, their laughter, their fights, their love. As a tear slipped down his cheek, he whispered, “We won every battle, Lina.” The wind carried his words across the lake, and for a fleeting moment, it felt as if she were there, smiling back.

Summary

In her final days, Lina surprises Dave by asking to fulfill his promise of skating. With her mother’s hesitant blessing, they head to the skate park, where Lina experiences the thrill of freedom one last time. However, the moment takes a tragic turn as her health falters, and she passes away shortly after. Dave, devastated, honors her memory by embracing the lessons she taught him, ensuring her spirit lives on in his actions.

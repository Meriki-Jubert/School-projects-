About

This is a story about Lina a girl fighting a battle with cancer and Dave a guy who lives life as a care free risk taker. Lina, who's life has been centered around care and quiteness meets Dave who's been living in total carelesness and noise. The two end up catching feelings for one another and learn the better side of their oposite lifes.

Lina

Reserved, thoughtful, battling cancer. Finds peace in books and quiet moments by the lake.

Dave

Carefree, energetic risk-taker. Lives for adrenaline, skateboarding, and making a splash.

Free Spirit

Lina arrived at the lake with a book in hand, feeling a mix of anticipation and disappointment when she didn’t see Dave. Just as she settled down, ready to read, a loud, distant chorus of voices calling her name made her freeze. Looking across the lake, she saw a group on a small boat—Dave and his friends. They waved enthusiastically, their voices carrying over the water. Flustered, Lina raised her book to cover her face, though she couldn’t hide the smile tugging at her lips. Dave, noticing her reaction, grinned. His plan was working. When the boat reached the shore, Dave hopped out first, dramatically dropping to one knee in front of her. Lina’s cheeks flushed. Was he… proposing? Her embarrassment turned to laughter as he removed his shoes, revealing how wet they were.

“I thought I’d make an entrance,” he said with a shrug. His friends surrounded Lina, bombarding her with random questions. Their lively energy was infectious, and soon they all settled down. Dave leaned closer to Lina. “Want a ride on the boat?” “I’ve never been on a boat,” Lina admitted. “I might get motion sickness.” Dave waved off her concerns, gently guiding her onto the boat. Despite her nerves, she found herself enjoying the gentle rocking of the water as Dave rowed them to the opposite shore. When they arrived, Dave laughed. “Finally, we’re rid of them.” Lina chuckled. “What now?” “Now?” Dave smirked. “We go wherever the wind takes us.” They walked for hours, chatting about everything and nothing. They eventually found a theme park but spent most of their time strolling through the grounds, too absorbed in each other to care about the rides.

By the time they returned to the lake, Dave’s friends had set up a campfire. The group shared stories, laughter filling the air. Dave told a tale of being kicked out of an office building for attempting to skateboard inside. The group roared with laughter. When the attention turned to Lina, she hesitated. “I don’t have any funny or embarrassing stories.” “Oh, come on,” Dave teased. “You should’ve seen your dad’s face when I told him how I got this,” he said, motioning to his bandaged hand. Lina rolled her eyes but relented, recounting the incident with humor. The group erupted in laughter. As the night wore on, Lina grabbed a can of beer from Dave’s hand, chugging it in one go before throwing her arms in the air with a triumphant scream. The others joined her, mimicking her celebration. For the first time in a long while, Lina felt alive.

When the group dispersed, only Lina and Dave remained by the campfire. They sat in comfortable silence, the warmth of the fire and the quiet of the night enveloping them. “The battle was excellent today” Dave said holding Lina in his arms. “I wish It could stay like this forever” Lina replied a bit cold but not sad, because today was a day recorded in her mind her forever. This this became a daily routine where Lina, Dave and his friends would hang out and for the last few week everything felt normal like it was all okay.

Summary

Dave surprises Lina with an impromptu day of adventures, taking her on a boat ride and walking aimlessly to a theme park. Their shared laughter and conversations highlight the joy they find in each other's company. That night, around a campfire with Dave's friends, Lina experiences a rare sense of freedom and warmth, creating memories she cherishes deeply.

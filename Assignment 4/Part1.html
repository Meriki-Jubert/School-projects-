About

This is a story about Lina a girl fighting a battle with cancer and Dave a guy who lives life as a care free risk taker. Lina, who's life has been centered around care and quiteness meets Dave who's been living in total carelesness and noise. The two end up catching feelings for one another and learn the better side of their oposite lifes.

Lina

Reserved, thoughtful, battling cancer. Finds peace in books and quiet moments by the lake.

Dave

Carefree, energetic risk-taker. Lives for adrenaline, skateboarding, and making a splash.

Opposites Meet

The sun hung low over the horizon, its golden light painting the surface of the lake with rippling hues of orange and pink. A gentle breeze rustled the trees as Lina sat on her favorite bench by the lake, a thick novel resting in her lap. The world around her seemed to fade as she lost herself in the story, her sanctuary from the relentless reality of hospital visits and the weight of her illness. She sighed, absently tugging the hem of her sweater. The faint hum of wheels on concrete reached her ears, a distant but familiar sound from the skate park nearby. It was a stark contrast to the tranquility of the lake, a mix of laughter, shouts, and clattering skateboards. Normally, she’d tune it out, but today, a particular voice caught her attention. “Hey, watch this!” someone yelled, followed by a loud cheer. Curious, Lina glanced up briefly, spotting a boy, probably around her age, leaping off a ramp with reckless confidence. His shaggy hair caught the fading sunlight as he landed with an unsteady wobble, grinning triumphantly at his friends. Lina shook her head, retreating back to her book. The world of fiction was far safer than whatever chaos that boy lived in. Dave kicked his skateboard to a stop, the adrenaline still buzzing in his veins. He loved the skate park, the freedom it offered, but something new caught his eye today. By the lake, a girl sat alone, completely engrossed in her book. She looked… different. Quiet. Almost untouchable in her stillness. Without thinking, Dave wandered closer to the lake, his curiosity piqued. When his eyes caught the shimmering water, an idea sparked in his mind. A grin spread across his face as he stripped off his shoes and shirt. “What are you doing, Dave?” one of his friends called out, but he waved them off. He sprinted to the water and dove in, making a loud splash. The cold hit him immediately, but he had a plan. Spluttering dramatically, he flailed his arms and shouted, “Help! I’m drowning!” From the corner of his eye, he saw the girl freeze, her book slipping from her hands. Her expression was a mix of horror and disbelief as she stood up, glancing around helplessly. Unable to keep the act up, Dave let out a burst of laughter and stood in the shallow water, perfectly fine. “You could have let me drown,” he called out, grinning. The girl’s face twisted in irritation. She bent to pick up her book, clearly unimpressed. “Next time, I will.” “Ouch. Harsh,” Dave said, swimming out of the water. He grabbed his shirt and slung it over his shoulder. “So, what’s your name?” She ignored him, walking away briskly. But Dave wasn’t one to give up easily.

Each day, he returned to the lake, trying a new approach. One day, he attempted a handstand on his skateboard, only to fall flat on his back. Another time, he brought a flower he picked from a nearby bush, offering it with a goofy grin. “Still no name?” he’d ask, every time met with silence or an exasperated shake of her head. It wasn’t until the fifth day that Lina finally broke. As Dave sat on the bench across from her, rattling off jokes, she sighed deeply and said, “Lina. My name is Lina.” Dave’s face lit up like the sun. “Lina. Nice to meet you. I’m Dave.” She rolled her eyes but couldn’t suppress the faint smile tugging at her lips. “Took you long enough to figure out how to have a normal conversation.” “I don’t do normal,” Dave said, settling in. “But I’m glad to finally meet you, Lina. So, what’s your story?” And just like that, hours slipped by as they talked. Dave, with his humor and wild stories, chipped away at Lina’s guarded demeanor. Lina, with her quiet wit and thoughtful insights, intrigued Dave in a way no one ever had. As the sun dipped below the horizon, painting the sky in deep purples and blues, a connection began to form, fragile but undeniable.

Summary

Dave, a carefree risk-taker, and Lina, a quiet girl battling cancer, cross paths at the lake by a skate park. Lina finds solace in her books, while Dave thrives on chaos. Their first meeting is marked by humor and persistence as Dave fakes drowning to get Lina's attention. Despite her initial reluctance, a budding friendship forms as they spend hours talking and learning about each other's worlds.

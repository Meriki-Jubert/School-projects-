Battles To Fight

Dave’s carefree life took a dark turn the night of the party. What began with laughter and music ended in chaos when a fight broke out. One of Dave’s friends was attacked, and without hesitation, Dave jumped in to defend him. In the heat of the moment, a bottle shattered, slashing across his hand. Blood poured from the wound as panic erupted around him. His friends rushed him to the hospital, where the doctor stitched his hand and insisted he stay overnight for observation. The atmosphere shifted when Dave’s father arrived, anger etched across his face. “What were you thinking?” his father yelled. “Do you enjoy putting yourself in danger?” Dave snapped, his own frustration boiling over. “I was trying to help my friend! What do you care? You’re not the man Mom loved anymore. She’d hate what you’ve become.” His father’s face fell, the anger replaced by a pained silence. He left the ward without another word, leaving Dave to grapple with his emotions alone. That night, tears streamed down his face as he sat in the dimly lit room, the weight of his words heavy on his chest. The next morning, Dave woke to find his father asleep beside him, his head resting on the bed. Tentatively, Dave tapped his shoulder. His father stirred, blinking awake. Without a word, Dave wrapped his arms around him. “I’m sorry,” Dave whispered. “I’ll be more careful. I promise.” His father held him tightly. “We can only control so much, Dave. Worrying won’t change anything. But we can try to do better.”

After being discharged, Dave left the hospital with his arm in a sling. On the way out, he spotted a familiar figure in the corridor—Lina, surrounded by her family and doctors. His heart sank at the sight of her pale face. Without hesitation, he walked into the room. “Hey, Lina. How are you feeling?” Lina’s parents turned to him, startled. Her father’s expression hardened. “Who are you?” “I’m Dave,” he said, offering a weak smile. “Lina’s friend.” At first, her parents seemed relieved she had made a friend, but their demeanor shifted when they noticed his bandaged hand. “What happened to you?” her mother asked sharply. “I got into a fight,” Dave admitted. Her parents exchanged wary glances, and her mother’s tone grew colder. “How did you meet our daughter, again?” Dave’s father, sensing the tension, stepped in and guided him out of the room. On the way home, his father asked questions, curious about the girl who had captivated his son’s attention.

Days passed, and Dave’s usual visits to the lake were met with emptiness. Then, one evening, he found Lina there, sobbing alone. He rushed to her, his heart breaking at the sight. “Lina, what’s wrong?” he asked, but she couldn’t respond through her tears. He knelt beside her, wrapping her in his arms. “I’m here. You’re not alone. I’ll hold you.” His words seemed to calm her.

Between shaky breaths, she whispered the devastating news. “The doctors said… the chemo isn’t working. I have a month left.” Dave’s world shattered. Tears brimmed in his eyes, but he quickly wiped them away, turning his head to hide his pain. Lina let out a soft laugh. “I’ve never seen you like this.” “I’ve only loved one person enough to make me like this,” he admitted, his voice trembling. “And it’s you.” Lina tried to brush it off, but his gaze didn’t waver. “I mean it, Lina. I love you.” Her eyes softened, and she finally confessed, “I love you too, Dave. But… time’s not on our side.” He nodded, determination flickering in his eyes. “We might lose the war against time, but we can win every battle until the end.”

Under the moonlight, he leaned in, and their lips met in a tender, bittersweet kiss. As they pulled apart, Dave smiled. “It’s late. You should head home. But remember, we have a battle to fight tomorrow.” Lina smiled through her tears. “I’ll be there.” And with that, she walked away, leaving Dave standing by the lake, ready to face whatever came next.

Summary

Dave's recklessness lands him in the hospital after a fight, straining his relationship with his father. Meanwhile, Lina faces worsening health. Despite these challenges, their friendship remains a source of solace. Dave's promise to always catch Lina takes on new meaning as their connection grows stronger, cementing their commitment to face life’s battles together.
